# Book A — Kids' Coloring Book (24 pages)

An original concept for young children, built from the owner's founding spec: the source listing that inspired it may inform **audience and broad theme only** — never its title, cover, characters, composition, or artwork. Every page is an original design.

## Audience and detail budget

- Ages ~4–8. One clear, friendly subject per page; minimal or no background clutter. A child should instantly recognize what the page is and want to color it.
- Age-appropriate always: nothing scary, no weapons, nothing a parent would side-eye.

## Line rules

- Stroke width: **5–6 pt at print size** (≈ 0.07–0.085 in) for main outlines; interior detail no thinner than 4 pt. Thick lines forgive shaky crayon control and define the "bold and easy" feel.
- Minimum colorable region: **~0.35 in** across — crayon-tip sized. No slivers, no tight double-line gaps under 0.15 in.
- Simple closed shapes only; generous rounded forms; no crosshatching or texture lines.

## Composition

- Subject fills 60–85% of the live area, centered or slightly high.
- White background. At most one or two simple grounding elements (a ground line, a sun, a cloud) when the page needs them.
- Consistency across all 24 pages: same character proportions, same eye/face conventions if characters recur, same line weight. Book A defines its own single visual style in its outline — hold it for all 24 pages.

## Structure

- 24 interior art pages, single-sided, per the KDP spec file.
- Front matter: title page, copyright, "This book belongs to ___" page.
- Cover: original direction, bright and simple, showing a colored sample of an interior-style subject.
