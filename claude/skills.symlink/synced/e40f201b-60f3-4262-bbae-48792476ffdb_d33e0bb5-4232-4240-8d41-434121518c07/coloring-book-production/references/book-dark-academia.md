# Book B — Dark Academia (adult, ~50 designs)

The flagship adult title, aimed at the dark academia aesthetic niche: scholarly, gothic, cozy-melancholy. The niche's buyers pay premium prices for atmosphere and line quality — this book competes on taste, not density.

## Mood and content

- The feeling: candlelit study at midnight, rain on leaded windows, a library that smells of old paper.
- **Zero vulgarity, zero spice, zero gore.** Skulls appear as vanitas still-life motifs (skull-and-flowers), never as horror. The book must stay giftable to anyone.
- No real universities' crests, mottos, or identifiable buildings — invented academia only.

## Subject bank (draw from, and extend in this spirit)

Library interiors and reading nooks · arched and rose windows · candlelit desks · stacks and shelves of books · vintage typewriters · quills, inkwells, wax seals, letters · botanical and anatomical study plates · Greek and Roman statuary · moths and beetles as specimen plates · skull-and-flower vanitas arrangements · celestial and astronomical charts · gothic architecture details (columns, arches, gargoyle-free tracery) · tea services with manuscripts · herbarium pages · pocket watches, keys, magnifying glasses · chess sets · globes and maps of invented places.

## Line rules

- Main strokes **2–3 pt** at print size; fine detail floor **1.5 pt** — nothing thinner, because hairlines vanish or break up in POD printing.
- All tone from line work: contour density, pattern fill (book spines, window panes, tiles), never gray, never solid-black areas beyond small accents (a raven's eye, not a raven).
- Minimum colorable region **~0.15 in** — pencil-tip sized; this is a colored-pencil audience.
- Moderate-to-fine detail, but every page keeps a few generous open regions so a session feels finishable.

## Composition

- Vary the framing across the book: full-scene interiors, single-object studies, specimen-plate layouts, decorative-border pages. Repetition of layout is the drift to watch for.
- White backgrounds; scenes end in deliberate vignette edges inside the live area, not at a hard crop.

## Structure and positioning

- ~50 single-sided designs; total ≤108 pages per the KDP spec file.
- Front matter includes intro + pen-test page.
- List price $10.99–$12.99. Keywords and categories aim at "dark academia coloring book" and "gothic coloring book."
- Book C is this book's sibling: shared world and motifs, **zero reused artwork** — reviewers punish recycled pages across a series.
