# Book C — Stained Glass Dark Academia Edition (adult, ~50 designs)

The same invented academia as Book B, seen entirely through leaded glass. Every page is composed as a window. Structurally this is also the series' "bold and easy" entry — the thick leading lines make it approachable for beginners, seniors, and casual colorists without saying so on the cover.

## The window grammar

- Every design is a window: rose windows, lancet (tall pointed) panels, round-arched panels, rectangular panel grids, quatrefoils, fan lights, tracery-topped compositions. Vary the window type across the book.
- A **consistent outer frame** system bounds every page — a double-rule window frame set just inside the live area. Same frame weights book-wide; the frame is the series signature.
- Inside the frame, the image is built from **glass segments**: the subject (a moth, a stack of books, a celestial chart) fractured into leaded pieces, with background glass filling the rest of the panel.

## Line rules (two-tier, non-negotiable for the style to read)

- **Leading (came) lines: 6–8 pt** at print size — these are the thick black lines between glass pieces, including the segment borders of the subject itself.
- **Interior detail lines: 2–3 pt** — sparing detail *within* a glass piece (a vein on a leaf, a line on a spine). Never let detail lines approach leading weight or the tiers collapse.
- Every glass segment is a fully closed region — inherent to the medium, and the reason this style de facto passes the closed-shapes ban.
- Minimum segment size **~0.25 in** across; ideal segments are finger-sized. Big segments are the point.

## Content

- Same subject bank and mood rules as Book B (`book-dark-academia.md` governs content); same zero-vulgarity, zero-real-IP, zero-real-institution rules.
- Translate, don't copy: a Book B subject may appear in Book C **only as a new composition** in window form. Zero reused artwork between the books.

## Composition

- The window fills most of the live area; pure white outside the frame.
- Background glass uses simple geometric leading (diamonds, rectangles, radiating bands) so the subject reads instantly at a glance — flip-through videos are the marketing channel, and the thumbnail read matters.

## Structure and positioning

- ~50 single-sided designs; total ≤108 pages per the KDP spec file.
- Front matter includes intro + pen-test page.
- List price $10.99–$12.99. Keywords aim at "stained glass coloring book" (high-traffic shelf) with dark academia as the differentiator, plus "gothic," "bold and easy," and "large print" where honest.
- KDP prints on standard paper only — never market a window-hanging/translucent effect; position pages as frame-worthy art.
